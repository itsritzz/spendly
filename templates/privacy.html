Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To authenticate you and display your personal expense history.
  • To generate summaries and breakdowns of your spending.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We do not integrate with any advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app. We will respond to all privacy-related inquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
